# One Line of Earned Pride

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557` (program/v1).

## The line

> The fisherman who mended nets for forty winters finally watched his son steer their boat home alone through the fog, and said nothing at all.

A quiet, earned moment: forty winters of mending nets, and the payoff is
not a speech or a trophy — it is a father watching his son bring the boat
home alone through the fog, and saying nothing at all. The pride is in the
scene, not in any word that names it.

## How it works

`main.py` is the entrypoint. It writes the line above — and nothing else —
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`). No
network, no randomness, no input files, no dependencies: deterministic,
exit 0.

## Run it locally

```sh
MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 -P -s main.py
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```
